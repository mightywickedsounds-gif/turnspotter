# Turn Spotter

Live crypto direction-prediction website for use alongside Trench.io, hosted on GitHub Pages from `main` (root): https://mightywickedsounds-gif.github.io/turnspotter/

- `index.html`: the whole website, a single self-contained page (no build step). Since round 35 the menu is **City** (default: 3D Bot City drawn by `city.js`, an ES module loaded only by that tab: streets, AI citizens, cars, a gas station, and the HQ penthouse where the boss's monitors mirror `paper/meme.json`; it shows every paper bot as its own company building, the scoreboard vs holding BTC, and the owner's three switches On/Off, Risk, Paper/Real saved to `paper/settings.json` via the GitHub API), **Live** (momentum meter on the exchange's trade stream, Multiverse fan, Match Trench's price feed picker). Since round 37 the rug check has no tab: it runs inside every meme bot. Since round 38 the city is a 15×15-block open world (pan/zoom, minimap, follow a citizen or car, drive your own car; the Rug Desk and the Arena stadium are buildings), and the City page has the Crew panel (`b.doing`), the pipeline strip (`funnel`), stat tags over buildings and the Team chat (`tape` entries `{t, bot, kind, txt, say}`). Off the menu but still in the file: the paste-a-coin Rug check card, the Bot Arena list and the **Scalper bot** (trades strong 3-minute swings on the coin on screen, out within a minute; `councilRulesSwing` + `bossRules`). The older market/practice/24-7 sections are hidden but still in the file. Pulls candles in the browser from Binance, then Binance US, then Coinbase. Model code lives between `/*LOGIC-START*/` and `/*LOGIC-END*/`. Calls come from the Experience brain (`experience`), logistic-regression weights in the `/*MODEL-START*/` block, written by `research/train.py` and retrained weekly by `.github/workflows/fetch-data.yml`. `learn` (the live learner) is shown for comparison only. Every bot trade goes past the table: `councilRules` (Critic) and `bossRules` (Boss), also applied in the `simRun` replay; the 3D table loads three.js from jsDelivr.
- `tradingview/TrenchAI_Direction.pine`: TradingView Pine v6 version of the Trend brain.
- `research/`: `data/*.csv.gz` holds 2023+ candles for 5 coins (with taker volume) and 45 days of 1-minute candles, refreshed weekly. `experiments3-5.py` and `train.py` work on that data; older experiments use `btc_1h_2011_2017.csv`. Always score with a one-candle entry delay too: Binance US bid/ask bounce inflates same-candle results.
- `paper/trader.mjs`: paper trader, run every 5 minutes inside ~5h40m jobs of `.github/workflows/paper-trade.yml`, which writes `paper/ledger.json`. The ledger's `status` field records live per-coin diagnostics. Since round 24 it pays the real spread and checks busts on 1-minute candles (version 1 archived in `ledger-v1.json`). `paper/trend.mjs` (trend bot: hold coins above their 50-day average, spot, no leverage) runs in the same loop and writes `paper/trend.json`. `paper/arena.mjs` (five 1-minute strategies on Coinbase BTC) has its own job, `.github/workflows/arena.yml`, and writes only `paper/arena.json`; each job saves only its own files. Short-term calls don't beat trading costs (`research/experiments7.py`); slow trend-following does (`experiments8.py`). The AI-calls trader is paused (`paused: true`, round 34: `research/experiments14.mjs`). `paper/meme.mjs` (own job `.github/workflows/meme.yml`, writes only `paper/meme.json`) runs a team of 16 paper bots (state v2: `bots{id:…}`, shared scan and `tape`; one strategy each in `STRATS`, incl. a random control) trading Solana meme coins that pass the rug checklist kept in `index.html` between `/*MEME-START*/` and `/*MEME-END*/` (RugCheck + DexScreener). Bots send phone alerts via ntfy.sh (`NTFY_TOPIC` in the workflows). `paper/echo.mjs` (round 39, run inside the `meme.yml` loop, writes only `paper/echo.json`) is Echo, a Bland AI phone voice that calls the owner with a 7:30 pm Central briefing and heads-up calls (quiet 10 pm–8 am, max 3/day); it needs the `BLAND_API_KEY` and `MY_PHONE` Actions secrets and never stores the number or transcripts in the public repo.
