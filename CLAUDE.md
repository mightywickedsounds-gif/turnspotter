# Turn Spotter

Live crypto direction-prediction website for use alongside Trench.io, hosted on GitHub Pages from `main` (root): https://mightywickedsounds-gif.github.io/turnspotter/

- `index.html`: the whole website, a single self-contained page (no build step). Since round 32 it shows three tabs: **Live** (momentum meter on the exchange's trade stream, Multiverse fan, Match Trench's price feed picker), **Arena** (leaderboard from `paper/arena.json`) and **Scalper bot** (trades strong 3-minute swings on the coin on screen, out within a minute; `councilRulesSwing` + `bossRules`). The older market/practice/24-7 sections are hidden but still in the file. Pulls candles in the browser from Binance, then Binance US, then Coinbase. Model code lives between `/*LOGIC-START*/` and `/*LOGIC-END*/`. Calls come from the Experience brain (`experience`), logistic-regression weights in the `/*MODEL-START*/` block, written by `research/train.py` and retrained weekly by `.github/workflows/fetch-data.yml`. `learn` (the live learner) is shown for comparison only. Every bot trade goes past the table: `councilRules` (Critic) and `bossRules` (Boss), also applied in the `simRun` replay; the 3D table loads three.js from jsDelivr.
- `tradingview/TrenchAI_Direction.pine`: TradingView Pine v6 version of the Trend brain.
- `research/`: `data/*.csv.gz` holds 2023+ candles for 5 coins (with taker volume) and 45 days of 1-minute candles, refreshed weekly. `experiments3-5.py` and `train.py` work on that data; older experiments use `btc_1h_2011_2017.csv`. Always score with a one-candle entry delay too: Binance US bid/ask bounce inflates same-candle results.
- `paper/trader.mjs`: paper trader, run every 5 minutes inside ~5h40m jobs of `.github/workflows/paper-trade.yml`, which writes `paper/ledger.json`. The ledger's `status` field records live per-coin diagnostics. Since round 24 it pays the real spread and checks busts on 1-minute candles (version 1 archived in `ledger-v1.json`). `paper/trend.mjs` (trend bot: hold coins above their 50-day average, spot, no leverage) runs in the same loop and writes `paper/trend.json`. `paper/arena.mjs` (five 1-minute strategies on Coinbase BTC) has its own job, `.github/workflows/arena.yml`, and writes only `paper/arena.json`; each job saves only its own files. Short-term calls don't beat trading costs (`research/experiments7.py`); slow trend-following does (`experiments8.py`).
