          How it works

          The Trend brain is a learning model that updates on every candle, looking at momentum, RSI, trend, volume and volatility. The Memory brain searches the loaded history for the 60 moments that looked most like right now and checks what happened next. A call only fires when the Trend brain is confident and the Memory brain agrees. If their recent calls drop under 52% right, calls pause until they recover.

          Backtested on 2013–2017 hourly Bitcoin data, calls were right about 59–61% of the time against a 52–53% baseline. Calls made right after price got stretched (bounce or roll-over setups) were right only 33–40% of the time, so the site skips them. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
