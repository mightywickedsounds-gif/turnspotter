          How it works

          Calls come from the Experience brain: a model trained on every 15-minute candle of all five coins since January 2023, retrained every week. It looks at recent moves, RSI, trend, where price sits in its daily range, volume and volatility. A call fires when its confidence passes your "How picky" setting.

          Tested on 16 months of data it had never seen (July 2025 to October 2026), calls were right 54–58% of the time, or 53–56% if you entered 15 minutes late. The main pattern it found: in today's market, short moves tend to partly reverse. The old live learner, which bet on moves continuing, was no better than a coin flip there. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
